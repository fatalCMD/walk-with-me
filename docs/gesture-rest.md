# Gesture rest poses

The relaxed revision uses nearly straight arms beside the thighs, soft elbows and
slightly wider wrists to clear hips. Companion alternates inward beckons; the inactive
arm keeps its rest pose. Lead, Rear and Natural keep their main motion.

Editable action names are WWM_Lead_RelaxedRest_v2, WWM_Companion_RelaxedRest_v2,
WWM_Rear_RelaxedRest_v2 and WWM_Natural_RelaxedRest_v2. The local Blender source is
`X:/!--- Main/Documents/Blender-Docs/walk-with-me-gestures-relaxed-v2.blend`.
Test transitions and walking in game; a valid export does not prove either.
