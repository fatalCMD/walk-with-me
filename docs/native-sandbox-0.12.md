# Sandbox 0.12

Rest opens a larger native activity area once, after 30 seconds by default.
There are no repeated radius steps or arranged circles. At this revision, nearby
radius defaulted to 80 and free-roam radius to 1000. Zero delay starts free roaming
immediately. Automatic rest keeps its 508-unit return range; explicit Relax stays.
Extra scripted poses are off by default. Furniture, meals and conversation still
depend on the location and Skyrim's activity selection.
