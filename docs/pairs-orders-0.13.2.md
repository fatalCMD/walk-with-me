# Pairs 0.13.2

The first available pair starts after three seconds of settling. Search visible
partners within 420 units instead of rejecting only the nearest one. Two companions
are enough. Separation over 500 units or sustained movement ends the pair.
Only the pair uses conversation packages for 18-30 seconds. Other followers continue
resting. Separate gesture queues prevent slow Papyrus delivery from losing a turn.
Combat, furniture, scenes and player dialogue take priority. No voiced lines added.
