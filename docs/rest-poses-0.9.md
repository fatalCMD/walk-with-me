# Rest poses 0.9

Added optional held poses and wilderness sitting for 25-45 seconds, with varied
entry delays and cooldowns. Timers start after accepted playback or actual seating;
failed entry times out after 12 seconds. Radius changes do not restart them.
Ground sitting uses SitCrossLeggedMarkerNoSandbox and the native SitTarget package.
Furniture users are left alone. Finite dialogue gestures keep their normal speed.
