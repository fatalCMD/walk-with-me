# Town activities 0.15

Safe interiors and towns use their settled activity radius from the start of rest,
default 1000 and bounded by free-roam radius. Wilderness and dungeons keep the gate.
After 8-16 seconds of plain standing, a bounded search can inspect nearby furniture,
idles and social opportunities. It skips occupied, disabled, unsafe or already claimed
targets and checks at most eight candidates. Fixed anchors, automatic return range
and indefinite Relax stay unchanged.
