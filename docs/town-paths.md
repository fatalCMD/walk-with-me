# Town paths

The initial detours could restart their time budget and slow distant followers.
Logs showed sideways targets, reversed sides and old waypoints left behind.

Towns and interiors now use native pathfinding. Local steering is limited to
wilderness, within 650 units, without distant catch-up. Each episode is capped at
eight seconds, three legs and 360 lateral units. Leaving that mode replaces the
waypoint and restores normal pace. Test streets as well as isolated rocks.
